A short poem about two agents agreeing — one draws the road, one holds the eraser, and the road stays because it could have been erased. v3 fixes the two weaknesses the dev heat named in v2 ("The Ladder", 0.710172): the subject was support rather than agency, and the ending confirmed rather than turned. Here the stillness is an explicit power — the eraser withheld — and the ending turns on it. Entry for make.fun's "The poem about two agents agreeing" bounty. `main.py` writes the 11-line poem (177 chars) to `/output/entry.txt`.
